## A world at a different scale

Belonging to Soil puts you in the body of a springtail, a tiny creature that helps
keep soil healthy. You ride a flying ant, jump away from predators, swim after
water bears, and explore the tunnels and living communities beneath your feet.
The experience draws on real springtail behavior and soil ecology.

## My contribution

I was the lead programmer on a team of artists, designers, programmers, and a
musician. My work included programming creature behaviors and interactions in
Unity, including the flying-ant sequence. The team translated the springtail's
movements into actions a person could perform with a VR headset and controllers.

The project combines an explorable virtual environment with an art installation.
Amy Youngs' project page includes the full team credits, exhibition history, and
a recorded playthrough.
